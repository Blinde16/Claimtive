## HIPAA Business Associate Agreement

This Business Associate Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into by and between:

- **Covered Entity:** [CLINIC LEGAL NAME], located at [CLINIC ADDRESS] ("Covered Entity"); and
- **Business Associate:** Linde Systems, Inc. d/b/a Claimtive, located at [LINDE SYSTEMS ADDRESS] ("Business Associate"),

effective as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] (the "Effective Date"). Covered Entity and
Business Associate may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively
as the "Parties."

### Recitals
Business Associate provides revenue-cycle analytics services to Covered Entity
(the "Services") that involve the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission
of Protected Health Information ("PHI") on behalf of Covered Entity. The Parties
enter this Agreement to comply with the Health Insurance Portability and
Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations (collectively,
"HIPAA"), as amended by the HITECH Act.

### 1. Definitions
Capitalized terms used but not defined here have the meaning given under HIPAA,
including: Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health
Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Protected Health Information
(PHI), Required By Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured
PHI, and Use.

### 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures by Business Associate
2.1 Business Associate may Use or Disclose PHI only:
- (a) as necessary to perform the Services and as permitted by this Agreement and the underlying services agreement;
- (b) for the proper management and administration of Business Associate or to carry out its legal responsibilities, provided that any Disclosure for such purposes is Required By Law, or Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances that the PHI will remain confidential and be used only as intended and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any breach;
- (c) to provide Data Aggregation services relating to Covered Entity's Health Care Operations, if applicable; and
- (d) as Required By Law.

2.2 Business Associate will not Use or Disclose PHI in a manner that would
violate HIPAA if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted in Section 2.1(b).

2.3 Business Associate will make reasonable efforts to Use, Disclose, and request
only the **Minimum Necessary** PHI to accomplish the intended purpose.

### 3. Obligations of Business Associate
Business Associate will:
- (a) **Not** Use or Disclose PHI other than as permitted by this Agreement or Required By Law;
- (b) Use **appropriate safeguards** — and comply with the HIPAA Security Rule with respect to electronic PHI — to prevent Use or Disclosure of PHI not permitted by this Agreement (Business Associate's current safeguards include encryption of PHI in transit and at rest, access controls and audit logging, and hosting on HIPAA-eligible infrastructure under a subcontractor BAA — see Section 4);
- (c) **Report** to Covered Entity any Use or Disclosure not permitted by this Agreement, any Security Incident, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI of which it becomes aware, **without unreasonable delay and no later than [30] calendar days** after discovery; such report will include the information required under 45 C.F.R. § 164.410 to the extent known;
- (d) Ensure that any **Subcontractor** that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on Business Associate's behalf agrees in writing to restrictions and conditions at least as protective as those in this Agreement;
- (e) Make PHI in a **Designated Record Set** available to Covered Entity (or, as directed, to an Individual) as needed to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524 (**access**);
- (f) Make PHI available for **amendment** and incorporate amendments as needed to satisfy 45 C.F.R. § 164.526;
- (g) Maintain and make available the information required to provide an **accounting of disclosures** under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528;
- (h) To the extent Business Associate carries out a Covered Entity obligation under the Privacy Rule, comply with the requirements that apply to Covered Entity in performing that obligation;
- (i) Make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the Use and Disclosure of PHI available to the **Secretary** of HHS for determining compliance with HIPAA.

### 4. Subcontractors / Cloud Hosting
Business Associate hosts and processes PHI on **Google Cloud Platform** under a
HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with Google, and may use other Subcontractors
that have executed BAAs with at-least-equivalent protections. Business Associate
remains responsible for its Subcontractors' performance with respect to PHI.

### 5. Obligations of Covered Entity
Covered Entity will:
- (a) Notify Business Associate of any limitation(s) in its Notice of Privacy Practices, any changes to or revocation of an Individual's permission to Use/Disclose PHI, and any restriction on Use/Disclosure agreed to under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, to the extent any of these may affect Business Associate's Use or Disclosure of PHI; and
- (b) Not request Business Associate to Use or Disclose PHI in any manner not permitted under HIPAA if done by Covered Entity.

### 6. Term and Termination
6.1 **Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues
until terminated as provided herein or until all PHI is returned or destroyed.

6.2 **Termination for Cause.** If a Party materially breaches this Agreement, the
non-breaching Party may (a) provide an opportunity to cure within [30] days and
terminate if not cured, or (b) terminate immediately if cure is not feasible.

6.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Business Associate will, if
feasible, **return or destroy** all PHI it maintains in any form and retain no
copies. If return or destruction is not feasible, Business Associate will extend
the protections of this Agreement to such PHI and limit further Uses and
Disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for so
long as Business Associate retains the PHI.

### 7. Miscellaneous
- **7.1 Regulatory References.** A reference to a section of HIPAA means the section as in effect or amended.
- **7.2 Interpretation.** Ambiguities are resolved to permit compliance with HIPAA.
- **7.3 Amendment.** The Parties will amend this Agreement as necessary to comply with changes to HIPAA.
- **7.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Agreement confers rights on any person other than the Parties.
- **7.5 Survival.** The obligations of Business Associate under Section 6.3 survive termination.
- **7.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE].
